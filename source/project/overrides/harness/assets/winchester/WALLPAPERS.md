# Winchester desktop wallpapers

Original images created with the built-in ImageGen tool on October 3, 2026. No Windows photographs or logos were copied. Full wallpapers are 1672 x 941 WebP assets; 320 x 180 thumbnails are separate small files for Settings. PNG generation originals are retained in the image-generation library. WebP conversion and thumbnail resizing do not change the artwork.

- `flow.webp`: Midnight Flow, the default. Deep blue sculptural folds, with a quiet left edge for shortcuts.
- `hills.webp`: Morning Hills. An uncluttered green landscape with a soft blue sky.
- `flow-thumb.webp` and `hills-thumb.webp`: Settings previews.

The wallpapers belong on the desktop. Welcome and setup surfaces use clear interface layouts instead of repeated wallpaper strips or filler photography.

## Final generation prompts

### Midnight Flow

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original high-quality widescreen 16:9 desktop wallpaper for Winchester OS, landscape 2560x1440 composition. Primary request: a refined blue abstract desktop background with the craft of a premium Windows wallpaper but an original design. One broad flowing sculptural silk-like folded form, gentle sweeping layers and deep spatial lighting, occupying the center-right of the frame. Leftmost quarter remains quiet dark navy with smooth low contrast so white desktop icons are easy to read. Background deep ink navy, form muted royal blue and glacier blue; no neon, no cyan glow, no oversaturation. Rich three-dimensional material with precise subtle highlights and soft shadows, clean studio-rendered finish, serene and restrained. The folds should be broad and calm rather than busy petals. Designed to sit behind readable interface windows. No logos, no Windows symbols, no writing, no frame, no UI, no watermark, no collage. Edge-to-edge landscape wallpaper only.

### Morning Hills

Create a premium original 16:9 desktop wallpaper photograph. Gentle smooth green rolling hills under a soft blue sky, two wisps of cloud at the far right. Calm classic Windows-style landscape composition, original contours rather than copying any photograph. Restrained colors, natural light, no rocky foreground, buildings, trees, people, writing, logos, UI or watermarks. Low detail on the left for readable white desktop icons.
